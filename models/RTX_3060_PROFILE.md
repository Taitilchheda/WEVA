## Verdict (real, not marketing)

| Model        | Recommended batch on RTX 3060 12GB | Why |
|--------------|------------------------------------|-----|
| WEVA-Tiny    | 64–96                             | tiny VRAM footprint, throughput is data-loader bound |
| WEVA-Small   | 32–48                             | sweet spot |
| WEVA-Medium  | 16–24                             | 60M is the **largest** that fits at batch 16; 12 GB is the hard ceiling |
| > 60M params | **Will not train on this GPU at any useful batch** | ADAM state alone is `2 × 4 bytes × params` = 0.5 GB for 60M; activations dominate at batch > 16 |

**So no, we have not trained 60M locally — and we cannot, at useful batch size, with this GPU.** The honest statement: **WEVA-Tiny and WEVA-Small run on the RTX 3060 12 GB at full batch; WEVA-Medium is at the edge with batch 16, and we have not yet trained it end-to-end (only the forward+backward is profiled, training is not finished).**

## Time estimates (1 epoch over 50K tuples, batch 32)

| Model | Step time | Steps | Epoch time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| WEVA-Tiny   | 130 ms | 1563 | 3.4 min  |
| WEVA-Small  | 564 ms | 1563 | 14.7 min |
| WEVA-Medium | batch 16: 836 ms × 2 = 1.67 s | 3125 | 87 min   |

So **one epoch of WEVA-Small on a 50K subset is ~15 minutes**. Twenty epochs: ~5 hours. That's a feasible overnight run. WEVA-Medium at batch 16 with grad-accumulation-2 is ~1.5 hours per epoch; twenty epochs is ~30 hours (1.3 days continuous).

## What to actually run

**Practical recommendation for the user:**

- **Default run:** WEVA-Small, batch 32, 50K subset, 20 epochs → ~5 hours, fits 12 GB.
- **Stretch run:** WEVA-Medium, batch 16 with grad-accum 2, 20 epochs → ~30 hours, also fits 12 GB.
- **Sanity check:** WEVA-Tiny, batch 64, full epoch → 3 min, easy to iterate.
